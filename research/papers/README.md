Priority reading for planning: P14 TimelyRAG, P12 Re³, P11 HoH and P13 Ragability establish close prior art and evaluation concerns; P08 ALCE and P10 PoisonedRAG address source support and attack risk. P01–P04 establish retrieval/NLI baselines. These works do not establish Cortex results.

No paper PDFs or copied full texts are committed. Link to publisher/arXiv records and honor redistribution terms. Before a reproduction, verify released code/data/model licenses and version hashes; distinguish venue publication year from preprint date. Abstract-level review supports scoped author claims, not full independent validation. Record deeper reading, negative results and changed interpretations in the research log.
