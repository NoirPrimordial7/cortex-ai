## Three original directions

1. **Evidence Studio — recommended for review.** Ink rail + warm white workspace, teal accents, source inspector alongside answers. Dense enough for enterprise work without packing everything into cards. Focus: selecting policy evidence and seeing why it applies.
2. **Policy Ledger.** Light graphite navigation, ivory document-first layout, amber validity accents and wide policy table. Focus: governance/version review; less assistant-centric.
3. **Signal Console.** Navy workspace, cyan controls and compact investigation layout. Focus: analyst inspection; darker visual weight and more contrast testing needed.

See [visual direction board](previews/visual-directions.png) and [moodboard](previews/moodboard.png). These are original conceptual boards with cited inspiration labels, not copied screenshots. Proposed selection is a design recommendation pending owner review; no implementation is authorized.

## Patterns selected and rejected

Use one typographic hierarchy, aligned grids, quiet separators, clearly named status labels and contextual evidence. Chat answer width stays approximately60–70 characters; long quotes remain readable. Document viewer uses text spans and version/date sidebar before introducing complex PDF overlays. Desktop1440 with224px rail; mobile390 stacks content and opens evidence below the answer. Microinteractions are feedback, not decoration. Primary theme is light; dark palette specified for later.

Reject excessive glow/gradients, animated 3D, invented confidence percentages, decorative metric charts with no decision value, hidden dates and unlabeled conflict badges. These are Cortex design judgments grounded in the product task, not claims that a reference site mandates them.
