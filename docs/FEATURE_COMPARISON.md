## Interpretation boundaries

- D-doc/field means read restrictions on indexed records/fields. Chunks inherit protection only if indexing and retrieval preserve policy. Separate section-level ACLs are NOT VERIFIED except where specifically documented, and not assumed from document permissions.
- D-EE means Onyx Enterprise Edition; D-selected/preview and beta are not uniform source support. RAGFlow resource scopes must not be mislabeled as inherited ACLs.
- P-filter means date/metadata primitives; it does not mean effective-date extraction, approved validity, historical as-of queries or partial supersession are solved.
- Conflict NV means no verified general-purpose detector in this review. It does not deny that a prompt or agent may compare documents. P-compose means a framework can host a detector that we must evaluate.
- Search hits and references are not automatically claim-level faithful citations. D-component is functionality to construct/parse citations, not demonstrated entailment.
- No 'best' overall competitor is ranked. Compare Cortex later with reproducible local retrieval baselines; paid platforms can remain documentation comparators unless access is authorized.
