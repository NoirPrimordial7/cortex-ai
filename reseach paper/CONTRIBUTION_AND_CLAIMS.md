# Contribution and claims

## Recommended title

Cortex AI: Permission-Gated Temporal Retrieval and Authority-Aware Conflict Abstention for Enterprise Policy Knowledge

## Contribution

Cortex AI implements and evaluates a bounded policy decision pipeline that combines document and tenant authorization, reviewed valid-time and scope predicates, expansion of comparable claims, strongest-tier conflict abstention, and version-bound citations. An Auditor can resolve an exact live conflict with a reversible, provenance-checked choice. The evaluated artifacts follow the selection through an actual API and an inspectable exact source. The contribution is the implemented integration and its reproducible evaluation.

## Scope of the title

Permission-gated refers to candidate eligibility before application evidence hydration, under the documented trust model. Temporal refers to reviewed effective intervals, not inferred dates or a bitemporal historical reconstruction. Authority-aware refers to the source-kind and rank rules plus the exact matching re-audit decision. Conflict is bounded to incompatible numeric reviewed values within the strongest applicable claim tier.
