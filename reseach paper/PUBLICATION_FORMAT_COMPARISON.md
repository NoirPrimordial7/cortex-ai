# Publication format comparison

| Edition | Basis | Layout and citations |
|---|---|---|
| Goa guidelines | [SMARTCOM public author guidance](https://www.smartcomconference.com/author-guidelines-for-paper-submission) | A4, one column, 12-point review layout and author–year citations. Editable Word and a matching proof. |
| Springer proceedings | [Springer Nature proceedings templates](https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/publish-a-book/step-by-step-conference-proceedings) | Official downloaded LLNCS class and numbered bibliography. Editable alternative uses the official Word template styles. |
| IEEE conference | [IEEE conference publication guidance](https://events.ieee.org/planning-basics/ieee-conference-publications/publishing-information-for-authors/) | Official IEEEtran conference class, two text columns, wide technical figures and sequential numbered citations. |
| IJSREM journal | [Author Submission Guidelines OP/61](https://ijsrem.com/uploads/issues/author_guidelines/1780278824993-f2f67bb9dd2b-IJSREM_Author_Submission_Guidelines_v2.pdf) and [engineering citation guide](https://ijsrem.com/uploads/issues/citation_guides/1780278824994-356a9d64c97f-IJSREM-Citation-Style-Guide.pdf) | A4 with one-inch margins, Times New Roman, two text columns, a centered title block, numbered Word equation and sequential IEEE citations. |
| General academic | Single-column article | Numbered figures and references with an editable LaTeX source. |

SMARTCOM identifies LNNS as the intended series. The organizer still needs to confirm template precedence, review anonymization and the applicable page-count convention. The Goa public guidance specifies at least 3,500 words or 10 pages, at most 250 abstract words and at most 50 references. [Conference requirements](CONFERENCE_REQUIREMENTS.md)

IJSREM's stated A4 size, one-inch side margins and 0.2-inch gutter leave about 3.03 inches per column. OP/61 separately lists 3.15 inches, so the Word proof uses the exact paper, margins and gutter and Word divides the remaining width. The proof uses wide sections at the point of discussion for the six diagrams, displayed equation and comparison table. Confirm wide figure placement against OP/61's top-or-bottom column guidance before journal submission. The local copy of OP/61 is stored in the ignored templates directory.

The supplied VERITAS PDF was inspected for compact two-column equation and diagram placement. The supplied Deepfake Forensics Tool article was inspected for IJSREM typography, diagram placement and captions. The current guidance governs the manuscript styles. Published article artwork, text, headers and identifiers were excluded.
